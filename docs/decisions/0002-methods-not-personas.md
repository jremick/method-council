# ADR 0002: Methods, not personas

- **Status:** accepted for Wave 0/1
- **Date:** 2026-08-19

## Decision

Every analysis pass applies a documented method with provenance,
applicability, procedure, failure modes, rigor variants, and a typed artifact.
The product does not simulate historical or living people.

## Consequences

- Selection is explained by analytical contribution, not reputation theatre.
- Synthesis reports convergence and divergence instead of weighted votes.
- ODNI and similar standards act as report-quality rubrics, not “members.”
- Public copy uses “adapted from” unless a narrower claim is directly sourced.
