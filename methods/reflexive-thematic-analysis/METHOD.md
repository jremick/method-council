# Reflexive Thematic Analysis

Status: **preview and unevaluated**

Use this method to interpret a bounded collection of interviews, documents, observations, or other
qualitative material for patterns of shared meaning. It moves recursively through familiarisation,
coding, theme development, review, definition, and reporting.

## What it adds

- source-linked codes and candidate themes;
- themes built around a central analytic idea rather than a topic count;
- visible analytic choices, revisions, contradictions, and alternative readings; and
- a reflexive record of how the model and human analyst shaped the interpretation.

## Important limit

An AI can assist with coding and candidate themes, but it cannot make the analysis objective, become
a participant, or replace an accountable qualitative researcher. Themes are interpretations, not
prevalence estimates or facts that simply emerge from data.

The canonical contract is [method.yaml](method.yaml). The adaptation follows public guidance from
[Braun and Clarke's Thematic Analysis site](https://www.thematicanalysis.net/doing-reflexive-ta/)
and remains an unevaluated preview.
