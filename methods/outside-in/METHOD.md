# Outside-In Context Scan

Status: **preview and unevaluated**

Use this method early when a question may be framed too narrowly around the team's existing system,
workflow, or assumptions. It starts with external forces and tests which ones materially change the
analysis, design, or decision.

## Procedure shape

1. Restate the issue without centring the current solution.
2. Identify plausible external forces across the relevant horizon.
3. Separate what is controlled, influenced, and external.
4. Trace material impact pathways.
5. Test relevance against evidence and preserve gaps.
6. Connect supported context to options, assumptions, and change conditions.

A long trend list is not a useful result. Factors should survive an impact and evidence test before
they shape the recommendation.

## Adaptation and limits

This is a domain-neutral adaptation of Outside-In Thinking in the CIA's public tradecraft primer. It
does not predict timing or magnitude without evidence and does not confer intelligence-community
compliance or endorsement. Independent fidelity and usefulness evaluation are still required.
