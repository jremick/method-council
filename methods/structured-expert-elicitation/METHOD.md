# Structured Expert Elicitation

Status: **preview and unevaluated**

Use this method to design or review a Delphi-style process with real experts. Participants first
answer independently, receive controlled anonymous feedback, and reconsider their judgments over
at least one further round. The result preserves distributions, rationales, dissent, and attrition.

## What it requires

- real consenting experts selected against documented criteria;
- a secure and appropriately anonymous participant process;
- at least two rounds;
- independent initial judgments and controlled feedback; and
- predefined aggregation, consensus, and stopping rules.

## Important limit

Multiple AI agents are not an expert panel. Consensus is not truth, calibrated probability,
stakeholder legitimacy, or independence from shared evidence.

The canonical contract is [method.yaml](method.yaml). It is a bounded adaptation of
[RAND methodological guidance for Delphi panels](https://www.rand.org/content/dam/rand/pubs/tools/TLA3000/TLA3082-1/RAND_TLA3082-1.pdf).
