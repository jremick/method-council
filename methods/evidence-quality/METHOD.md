# Evidence Quality Review

Status: **preview**

Use this method before synthesis when source quality, coverage, correlation, or currency could
change the judgment. It inventories the evidence, isolates load-bearing sources, records gaps and
contradictions, and explains the resulting confidence boundary.

## Procedure shape

1. Inventory stable evidence references and access limits.
2. Assess evidence critical to each judgment.
3. At standard depth, trace common origin and circular citation.
4. Record gaps, ambiguity, and affected judgments.
5. Explain the confidence contribution and, at intensive depth, refresh triggers.

The artifact should preserve `fact`, `inference`, `assumption`, and `unknown` separately. A URL,
citation, or authoritative publisher is not itself proof of a claim.

## Adaptation and limits

This is a domain-neutral adaptation of the CIA primer's Quality of Information Check, informed by
ODNI's public analytic standards. It adds model-era concerns such as retrieval limits and source
correlation. It is not an implementation of classified tradecraft, an ICD 203 conformance check, or
an endorsement by either organisation. Independent fidelity review is still required.
