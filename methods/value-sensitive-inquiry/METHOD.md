# Value-Sensitive Inquiry

Use this method when a system or decision may affect people differently and the design choices
carry values, rights, responsibilities, power, or access consequences.

It asks three connected questions:

1. **Conceptual:** who is affected and which values may be at stake?
2. **Empirical:** what do actual stakeholder or user evidence and observed practice show?
3. **Technical:** how do the system's design choices support, constrain, or reshape those values?

## What it produces

- a map of direct and indirect stakeholders;
- source-bound value and stakeholder claims;
- links between technical choices and possible value effects;
- visible tensions, distributional effects, and missing voices; and
- the next empirical or technical inquiry needed before the decision can be completed.

## Important limit

An AI cannot consult people, represent them, or create consent. Generated stakeholder views are
hypotheses, not evidence. If material stakeholder evidence is missing, the result must stay
`INCOMPLETE` and say which participation or research is still needed.

The canonical contract is [method.yaml](method.yaml). The adaptation is based on the public
[Value Sensitive Design Lab overview](https://vsdesign.org/vsd/); it remains an unevaluated preview.
