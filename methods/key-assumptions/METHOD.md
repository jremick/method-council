# Key Assumptions Check

Status: **preview**

Use this method when a judgment, recommendation, or design claim depends on premises that may be
implicit or stale. State the judgment, expose what must be true, challenge each load-bearing
assumption, and record how failure would alter the conclusion.

## Procedure shape

1. State a falsifiable judgment and its boundary.
2. List stated and unstated premises.
3. Separate load-bearing assumptions from contextual factors and unknowns.
4. Challenge validity, currency, and counterevidence.
5. Record impact and observable change conditions.

An assumption is not disproved merely because it was challenged. Missing evidence remains an
unknown, and a repeated same-model pass is correlated rather than independent review.

## Adaptation and limits

The structure is adapted from the CIA's public Key Assumptions Check. Method Council adds typed
findings, evidence binding, and explicit change conditions. The preview cannot prove that all hidden
assumptions were found and does not imply CIA training, certification, or endorsement.
