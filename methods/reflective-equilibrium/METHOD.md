# Reflective Equilibrium

Status: **preview and unevaluated**

Use this method when a question cannot be settled by factual evidence alone because judgments about
cases, values, duties, or fairness conflict with broader principles. It iterates between judgments,
principles, arguments, and cases while recording every material revision.

## Procedure shape

1. Bound the normative question and accountable decision authority.
2. Record supplied considered judgments and their conditions.
3. State candidate principles and their sources.
4. Test the fit using representative and difficult cases.
5. Revise judgments, principles, or scope explicitly.
6. Record the current coherent position, dissent, tensions, and revision triggers.

At intensive depth, the method also compares background theories and tests bias, exclusion, and
source independence.

## Adaptation and limits

This is a bounded project adaptation of reflective equilibrium. Coherence is not moral truth,
democratic legitimacy, or stakeholder consent. The AI must not invent considered judgments or
pretend that a tidy synthesis resolves genuine value conflict.
