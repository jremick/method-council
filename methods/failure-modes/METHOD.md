# Failure Modes Review

Status: **preview**

Use this method for a preliminary bottom-up review of how defined system elements and interfaces
could fail, how effects propagate, what controls exist, and what follow-up is needed.

## Procedure shape

1. Bound the system and decompose functions, elements, and interfaces.
2. Identify failure modes without conflating cause, mode, and effect.
3. Trace local and system effects.
4. Record detection, prevention, compensation, containment, and recovery controls.
5. At intensive depth, examine common causes and separately justified risk dimensions.
6. Assign actions, verification plans, and explicit coverage limitations.

This catalog intentionally omits proprietary forms and numerical criticality formulae. A
model-generated review is not evidence of safety, reliability, or hazard closure.

## Adaptation and limits

The workflow adapts public NASA FMEA guidance into a lightweight cross-domain review. NASA marks
GSFC-HDBK-8004 as active and publicly accessible, but not mandatory, not a NASA core standard, and
not NASA-endorsed. This preview is not a validated FMEA/FMECA implementation and must not replace
qualified safety, reliability, software assurance, or regulatory work.
