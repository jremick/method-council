# Concept and Meaning Clarification

Status: **preview and unevaluated**

Use this method when an important question depends on a vague, overloaded, or contested term. It
separates documented uses, proposes a clearer purpose-fit concept, tests boundary cases, and records
what the clarification preserves or changes.

## Procedure shape

1. Name the term, context, and purpose of clarification.
2. Collect actual source-bound uses where the selected rigor requires them.
3. Separate materially different senses or levels.
4. Propose a more precise concept without calling it the uniquely correct meaning.
5. Test positive, negative, borderline, and adversarial cases.
6. Record changed classifications, excluded uses, value choices, and unresolved meaning.

Precision can improve a discussion without settling it. A result should remain `INCOMPLETE` when
the actual uses or affected contexts are unavailable.

## Adaptation and limits

This is a project adaptation of Carnapian explication informed by contemporary discussion of
conceptual engineering. It is not an official philosophical standard, and it cannot establish one
universal definition. A proposed concept may encode values or exclusions even when its wording is
technically precise.
