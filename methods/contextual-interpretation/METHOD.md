# Contextual Interpretation

Status: **preview and unevaluated**

Use this method when a text, statement, policy, action, or artifact can only be understood by
considering its parts, its wider whole, and its historical or institutional context. It preserves
credible alternative readings rather than forcing a single interpretation.

## Procedure shape

1. Bound the material and the interpretation question.
2. State the initial reading and the assumptions brought to it.
3. Move between decision-relevant parts and the wider whole.
4. Reconstruct only source-supported context.
5. Compare and test credible readings.
6. Record changed understanding, unresolved alternatives, and missing material.

The method should remain `INCOMPLETE` when primary material or material context is unavailable.

## Adaptation and limits

This is a bounded project adaptation of selected hermeneutic commitments. Hermeneutics is a diverse
and contested tradition, not one standard algorithm. The method cannot recover private intent,
invent historical context, or turn a plausible interpretation into factual proof.
