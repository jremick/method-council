# Adaptive Management

Status: **preview and unevaluated**

Use this method when a consequential decision can be revised and monitored action can reduce an
uncertainty that matters. It links objectives, alternatives, predictions, monitoring, review rules,
learning, and authorised adjustment in a repeated cycle.

## What makes it adaptive

- competing models or assumptions produce testable predictions;
- monitoring is designed for a later decision, not merely reporting;
- review dates, stop conditions, and possible responses are agreed in advance;
- an accountable authority can actually change course; and
- completed cycles preserve evidence, dissent, harms, and remaining uncertainty.

## Important limit

A plan, experiment, or dashboard is not adaptive management by itself. The method requires lawful
authority, sustained monitoring, acceptable harm boundaries, and evidence from completed cycles for
any claim that learning or adaptation occurred.

The canonical contract is [method.yaml](method.yaml). It is a domain-neutral project adaptation of
public U.S. Department of the Interior [adaptive-management policy](https://www.doi.gov/media/655)
and technical guidance.
