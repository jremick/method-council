# Rights and Proportionality Review

Status: **preview and unevaluated**

Use this method when a policy, design, or rule may limit an established right or comparably
important interest. It asks whether the objective is sufficiently important, the measure is
rationally connected to it, less-restrictive alternatives exist, and the overall balance is defensible.

## What it produces

- a bounded account of the right, authority, objective, and proposed limit;
- evidence for and against the measure's connection to its objective;
- a comparison with feasible less-restrictive alternatives;
- visible distribution of benefits and burdens; and
- a reasoned judgment, contrary view, monitoring needs, and change conditions.

## Important limit

This is not legal advice. Applicable rights, legal tests, evidence burdens, and remedies depend on
the jurisdiction and responsible authority. A model cannot adjudicate the question or turn a
balancing exercise into legitimacy.

The canonical contract is [method.yaml](method.yaml). It is a general project adaptation informed
by the Department of Justice Canada's public [Charterpedia guidance on reasonable limits](https://www.justice.gc.ca/eng/csj-sjc/rfc-dlc/ccrf-ccdl/check/art1.html).
