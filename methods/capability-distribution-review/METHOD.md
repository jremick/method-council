# Capability and Distribution Review

Status: **preview and unevaluated**

Use this method when equal resources, access, or formal rights may still leave people with very
different real opportunities. It distinguishes what people receive from what they are actually
able to do or be with it.

## What it examines

- resources and formal entitlements;
- substantive opportunities and achieved outcomes;
- personal, social, institutional, technical, and environmental conversion factors;
- constrained or mutually incompatible choices; and
- how gains and burdens are distributed across different people and groups.

## Important limit

The capability approach does not provide one universal list or a complete theory of justice.
Capability selection and weighting need explicit justification and real evidence about affected
people. The model must not invent their values, agency, constraints, or preferences.

The canonical contract is [method.yaml](method.yaml). It is a bounded adaptation informed by the
Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy overview of [The Capability Approach](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/capability-approach/).
