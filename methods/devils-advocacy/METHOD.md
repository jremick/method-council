# Devil's Advocacy

Status: **preview**

Use this method to stress-test one dominant judgment, consensus, or load-bearing assumption. It
constructs the strongest credible contrary case, then records what survives and what must change.

## Procedure shape

1. Freeze the prevailing view, assumptions, evidence, and confidence basis.
2. Select a materially important challenge target.
3. Audit evidence and build a steelmanned alternative.
4. Test the mainline case; at intensive depth, seek a bounded rebuttal.
5. Record whether the judgment is retained, caveated, revised, or incomplete.

The alternative case is explicitly labelled advocacy. Its strength does not establish its truth,
and a same-model challenge is correlated rather than independent validation.

## Adaptation and limits

The workflow follows the CIA primer's public Devil's Advocacy structure and adds typed evidence,
correlation labels, and a disposition contract. It does not claim CIA training, endorsement,
certification, or independent fidelity validation.
