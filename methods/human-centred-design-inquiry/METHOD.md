# Human-Centred Design Inquiry

Status: **preview and unevaluated**

Use this method when a product, service, workflow, or interactive system must work for real people
in real contexts. It separates evidence-backed user needs from internal assumptions and proposed
features, then connects those needs to requirements, prototypes, evaluation, and iteration.

## What it requires

- actual user or service evidence;
- users, non-users, support roles, tasks, environments, and barriers;
- traceable needs and testable requirements;
- evidence from prototypes or existing designs; and
- a plan for the next user-research and design cycle.

## Important limit

AI-generated personas are not users, participation, or user research. This method does not establish
usability, accessibility, safety, inclusion, adoption, or ISO conformance.

The canonical contract is [method.yaml](method.yaml). The adaptation uses the public
[ISO 9241-210 overview](https://www.iso.org/standard/77520.html) only for its lifecycle boundary and
public [GOV.UK user-needs guidance](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard/point-1-understand-user-needs)
for practical evidence and iteration.
