# Theory of Change

Status: **preview and unevaluated**

Use this method to explain how a programme, policy, service, or planned change is expected to work.
It connects a defined problem and available inputs to activities, mechanisms, outputs, outcomes, and
longer-term impacts.

## What it makes visible

- the intended causal pathway and its weakest links;
- assumptions, preconditions, risks, context, and unintended effects;
- supporting and contrary evidence for each important link;
- alternative mechanisms or interventions; and
- indicators and review points that could strengthen, weaken, or revise the theory.

## Important limit

A coherent map is a hypothesis about change, not proof of causation or attribution. Real stakeholders,
domain experts, implementation evidence, and outcome evaluation remain necessary.

The canonical contract is [method.yaml](method.yaml). It adapts public UK government guidance from
the [Magenta Book](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-magenta-book/magenta-book-central-government-guidance-on-evaluation-html)
and the [Government Analysis Function toolkit](https://analysisfunction.civilservice.gov.uk/policy-store/the-analysis-function-theory-of-change-toolkit/).
