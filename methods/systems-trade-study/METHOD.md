# Systems Trade Study

Status: **preview**

Use this method to compare feasible system alternatives against a shared decision frame. It keeps
requirements, constraints, criteria, measures, uncertainty, recommendation, and final decision
separate.

## Procedure shape

1. State the decision, owner, outcome, boundary, and constraints.
2. Define alternatives at comparable resolution.
3. Establish criteria before evaluation.
4. Apply consistent measures and preserve unknowns.
5. Test sensitivity and, at intensive depth, challenge the comparison.
6. Record the recommendation separately from the accountable decision.

Weighted scoring is optional. It cannot turn subjective criteria into facts or safely average away
a non-negotiable constraint.

## Adaptation and limits

This workflow adapts NASA's public decision-analysis guidance into a portable tool contract. It is
not a NASA process implementation, does not satisfy programme-specific requirements, and has not
yet received independent fidelity review.
